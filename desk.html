🧭 The Desk

what this thing actually is — the lanes, the gates, and the calls it refused

This is a paper research desk. It reads markets, forms directional views, journals every one of them, and grades them later against a benchmark. No funds move. No wallet is connected to any of the numbers on this page. The point is not to look smart — it is to find out, with a real sample, whether the calls are worth anything.

Every figure below is read from the desk's own stores, not typed by hand. If a lane has no evidence, it says so.
